Patient Checklist

What Information
You Need

The pipeline runs on your molecular data. Most patients never know they can request these files — they are legally yours.

🧬
Whole Genome / Exome Sequencing
Critical
  • WGS of tumor tissue — the gold standard
  • WES as minimum, 5+ providers if possible
  • Tumor + matched normal (blood) paired
  • Raw FASTQ or VCF files — not just PDF reports
How to get it: Request raw sequencing files from your hospital. HIPAA makes them legally yours. If they refuse, order directly from Personalis, Tempus, or CeGaT.
🔭
Single-Cell RNA Sequencing
Critical
  • scRNA-seq of fresh tumor biopsy — not FFPE
  • Demand cryopreservation before every biopsy
  • PBMC collections at multiple timepoints
  • H5AD or raw 10x Genomics format
Why it matters: This is how FAP was found — invisible to every standard panel. Without it, the radioligand therapy arm doesn't exist.
🩸
Liquid Biopsy (ctDNA)
Critical
  • Signatera (Natera): personalized ctDNA tracking
  • Northstar: methylation-based signal
  • Every 2–4 weeks during treatment, not quarterly
  • Multiple platforms for cross-validation
Real-time feedback: ctDNA confirmed treatment response weeks before imaging. Prevents wasted months on failing therapy.
🧪
HLA Typing
Important
  • High-resolution Class I (A, B, C alleles)
  • Class II (DR, DQ) for CD4+ T cell vaccines
  • Can be derived from WGS via OptiType
  • Every neoantigen prediction is HLA-specific
Pipeline requirement: Without your HLA type, neoantigen binding predictions are impossible.
📊
Bulk RNA-seq
Important
  • Bulk RNA-seq of tumor tissue at minimum
  • Multiple providers across timepoints
  • TPM/FPKM counts matrix — not a report
  • Filters neoantigens to expressed genes only
Reduces false positives: A mutation in an unexpressed gene can't form a neoantigen. Expression data cuts prediction noise.
🖼️
Imaging & Pathology
Supplementary
  • PET/CT in DICOM format, not just a radiology report
  • Novel tracers: 68Ga-FAP, 68Ga-B7H3, 68Ga-EphA2
  • H&E and IHC slides with immune staining
  • Flow cytometry: B/T cell subsets over time
Theranostics: 68Ga-FAP PET positive = green light for radioligand therapy. Same molecule, different payload.

Under the Hood

The 6-Phase Pipeline

Open-source, auditable, runs on a consumer GPU. Every output is reviewable by you and your oncologist.

01
Data Acquisition
Downloads pre-called variants, RNA-seq, and scRNA-seq from your data source or public repos (osteosarc.com, GEO, TCGA).
gsutilGEOparse
02
Variant Annotation
Annotates somatic variants with gene symbols and protein changes. Falls back to pyvcf if VEP unavailable.
VEPpyvcf3pysam
03
scRNA-seq Target Discovery
QC, normalization, UMAP clustering, differential expression. Checks for FAP, B7-H3, EphA2, and 8 other surface targets. Outputs cluster plots and ranked target list.
ScanpyLeidenWilcoxon
04
Neoantigen Prediction
Predicts MHC Class I binding for all somatic peptides against your HLA alleles. Top 50 candidates selected by IC50 <500nM threshold.
pVACseqMHCflurryVaxrank
05
Structural Validation
Validates each candidate via ESMFold API. Computes mean pLDDT as a foldability proxy. Tiers candidates T1–T4. T1 (pLDDT >85) = priority vaccine targets.
ESMFoldColabFoldAlphaFold
06
Treatment Recommendation
Generates a structured report: 5-layer parallel treatment strategy, confirmed targets, ranked candidates, monitoring protocol, and regulatory access paths for every drug.
PythonPandasMarkdown
