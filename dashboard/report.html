    → FDA approved (combination with PD-1 inhibitor)
    
                  
    
                
    
              
    
              
    
                Access path: Both are FDA-approved. If your oncologist won't prescribe off-label,
                file FDA Form 3926 for Individual Patient Expanded Access. Approval typically within 48 hours.

    AdaPT-001 (TGF-beta trap adenovirus)
    
                    
    Infects and kills cancer cells while trapping TGF-beta. Releases tumor antigens to prime further immune response. Intratumoral or subcutaneous.

    SNK-01 (NK cell therapy)
    
                    
    Off-the-shelf NK cell therapy. Innate immune killing is MHC-independent — works even when tumor cells downregulate MHC to escape T cells.
    
                    
    → FDA Form 3926. NantKwest. Works synergistically with Anktiva (IL-15 superagonist).
    
                  
    
                
    
                
    
                  ⬆️
                  
    
                    
    Anktiva (N-803) — IL-15 superagonist
    
                    
    Amplifies NK and T cell proliferation. Use alongside SNK-01 to boost the NK cell response. Also enhances vaccine-driven T cell expansion.
    
                    
    → FDA approved (bladder cancer). Off-label via Form 3926 for osteosarcoma.
    
                  
    
                
    
              
    
              
    
                Support: XGeva (denosumab) for bone protection — critical in osteosarcoma to prevent pathological fractures and osteoclast-mediated bone destruction.
