What Happens Next
After submission, here's the pipeline flow:
Phase 1–2: Variant download and annotation (~1–2 hours)
Phase 3: scRNA-seq target discovery — checks for FAP, B7-H3, EphA2 and 8 others
Phase 4–5: Neoantigen prediction + structural validation via ESMFold (~$5–15 cloud cost)
Phase 6: Full treatment recommendation report with regulatory access pathways
Estimated total time: 24–48 hours. You'll receive your report at the email you provided.
⚠️
Medical disclaimer: This pipeline generates decision-support information only. All treatment decisions must be reviewed with a qualified oncologist familiar with your full clinical history. Expanded access applications require physician co-signing.
